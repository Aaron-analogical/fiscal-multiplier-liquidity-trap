How financing changes the fiscal multiplier

A government spends one more dollar during a slump. How much does GDP rise? This page lets you explore the answer from my M.Sc. thesis (HEC Montréal, 2023), which compares paying with a lump-sum tax, a labor tax, or debt, in normal times and when interest rates are stuck at zero (a liquidity trap).
